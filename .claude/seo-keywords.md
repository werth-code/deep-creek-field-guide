# Keyword targets — Deep Creek Field Guide

Audited 4 August 2026. No Ahrefs/Semrush connected, so demand is judged from
SERP composition and competitor behaviour rather than volume figures. Treat the
rankings below as directional.

## The one finding that matters

**The site targets "Garrett County". People search "Deep Creek Lake."**

| Term | In title/meta/h1 | Should be |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Creek Lake | 4 of 33 pages | 15+ |
| Garrett County | 11 of 33 | keep, secondary |

Garrett County is the administrative name. Deep Creek Lake is what's on the
sign, in the rental listing, and in the search box. Everyone competing for this
traffic (TripAdvisor, RVshare, Outdoorsy, Red Barn Vacations, the Chamber) leads
with the lake. We lead with the county, which is correct and invisible.

Fix: the lake becomes the primary geographic anchor. The county stays as the
precision signal, because precision is the product.

## Question-shaped — where the site already wins

These match how people actually type, and we hold verified answers for them.
Under-used in titles today.

- do you need a reservation for swallow falls
- is there a fee for deep creek lake state park
- does deep creek lake state park have a beach
- are dogs allowed at swallow falls
- what time does swallow falls open
- where can you swim in deep creek lake
- rock maze trail garrett county

**"Swallow falls reservations" is the strongest opportunity on the site.** The
rule changed in May 2026, most published answers are wrong, and we have it
right with sources. Low competition, high consequence, and it is exactly the
query a person types the night before driving three hours.

## Under-targeted own content

1. **Rock Maze** — DNR doesn't name it, so there is almost no competing content.
   We have photographs and a trailhead description. Nobody else does.
2. **Restrooms** — "does X have restrooms" is high-intent and nobody publishes
   it. We now have it for 8 of 10 town parks.
3. **Boat rentals** — confirmed at Herrington Manor. One competing page.
4. **Free parks** — "free things to do deep creek lake" has demand; we know
   which parks charge nothing.

## Writing rules

- Lake first, county second, in that order, in titles.
- Keep the question-shaped h1s. Don't sacrifice voice for a keyword.
- Never keyword-stuff a fact. "Reservations required weekends and holidays"
  stays exactly that precise.
- Alt text on every photo names the place. Currently good.
